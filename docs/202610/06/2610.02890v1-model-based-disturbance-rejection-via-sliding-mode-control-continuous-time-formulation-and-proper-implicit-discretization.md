## 摘要
我们提出了一种用于参考跟踪的鲁棒精确滑模控制器，该控制器具备基于模型的扰动抑制功能。其中，扰动由受有界输入驱动的线性时不变模型生成，并可能与受控对象存在双向耦合。该控制器嵌入了内模原理，其误差动力学与鲁棒精确微分器相镜像，从而确保了有限时间收敛。我们为采样数据实现开发了一种适当的隐式离散化方法，以保持系统的稳定性和精度。在包含由 $p$ 阶积分器链生成的扰动的标称情况下，该方法实现了 $T^{p+1}\bar D$ 的控制精度，其中 $T$ 为离散化时间，$\bar D$ 为扰动界限。研究表明，该精度与基本下界的关系仅取决于扰动模型的阶数。仿真结果验证了所提方法的有效性。

## Abstract
We propose a robust exact sliding-mode controller for reference tracking with model-based disturbance rejection, where the disturbance is generated by a linear time-invariant model driven by a bounded input and may be bidirectionally coupled with the plant. The controller embeds the internal model principle and its error dynamics mirror those of the robust exact differentiator, ensuring finite-time convergence. We develop a proper implicit discretization for sampled-data implementation that preserves stability and accuracy. In the nominal case, which contains a disturbance generated by a $p$-th order integrator chain, the method achieves a control accuracy of $T^{p+1}\bar D$, with discretization time $T$ and disturbance bound $\bar D$. This accuracy is shown to relate to the fundamental lower bound by a factor depending only on the order of the disturbance model. Simulations demonstrate the efficacy of the proposed methods.
