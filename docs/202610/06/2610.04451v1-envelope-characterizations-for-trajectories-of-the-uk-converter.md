## 摘要
本文在时间依赖和状态依赖切换的情况下，对Ćuk变换器轨迹的稳定性及包络线边界进行了完整表征。具体而言，建立了差分方程用于计算系统响应包络线的边界，并构造了李雅普诺夫函数以证明系统在连续和断续两种工作模式下的稳定性。每种情况均通过多次仿真进行了说明。

## Abstract
In this paper, a complete characterization of the stability and the boundaries of the envelopes for trajectories of the Ćuk converter is provided in the cases of time and state-dependent switching. In particular, difference equations are developed and used to compute boundaries for the system's response envelopes as well as Lyapunov functions to show the system's stability in both continuous and discontinuous regimes of operation. Each case is illustrated by multiple simulations.
