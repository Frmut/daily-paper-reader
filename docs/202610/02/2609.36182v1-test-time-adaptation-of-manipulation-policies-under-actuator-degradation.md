## 摘要
机器人操作策略通常基于以下假设进行训练：即指令动作在训练后即使经过长时间运行，仍能产生与训练时相同的运动。然而，真实硬件违背了这一假设，因为电机在运行中会逐渐发热、电流在接触附近趋于饱和、电压在负载下下降，导致相同的策略动作可能产生更弱、延迟或噪声更大的运动。这些状况通常已由机载遥测系统（如关节温度、电机电流和电源电压）测量，但这些信号通常仅用于记录或安全检查，而非策略自适应。我们引入了遥测感知动作校正（TeAR），这是一种与策略无关的方法，通过在输出动作到达底层控制器之前对其进行修正，将冻结的操作策略转化为遥测条件策略。TeAR 学习了一个轻量级 Transformer，它将建议动作与实时执行器遥测数据相结合，并对各个动作分量进行放大、阻尼或偏差调整。我们在涵盖 8 个策略家族和 5 个操作任务的 18 个策略-任务对上评估了 TeAR。在退化模型不匹配的额外配对评估中，TeAR 达到了 31.8% 的成功率，而基础策略为 25.6%，假设模型逆向方法为 30.6%。在物理机械臂上，TeAR 在无需机器人在线微调的情况下，将发热条件下的成功率提高了 10-15%。

## Abstract
Robot manipulation policies are usually trained under the assumption that a commanded action produces the same motion as it did during training even after hours of operation. Real hardware violates this assumption as the motors gradually heat up, current saturates near contact, voltage sags under load, thus the same policy action can produce a weaker, delayed, or noisier motion. These conditions are already measured by onboard telemetry, such as joint temperature, motor current, and supply voltage, yet this signal is typically used only for logging or safety checks rather than policy adaptation. We introduce Telemetry-Aware Action Rectification (TeAR), a policy-agnostic method that turns a frozen manipulation policy into a telemetry-conditioned policy by rectifying its outgoing action before it reaches the low-level controller. TeAR learns a lightweight Transformer that combines the proposed action with live actuator telemetry and amplifies, damps, or biases individual action components. We evaluate TeAR across 18 policy-task pairs spanning 8 policy families and 5 manipulation tasks. In an additional paired evaluation with degradation-model mismatch, TeAR achieves 31.8% success, compared with 25.6% for the base policy and 30.6% for an assumed-model inverse. On a physical arm, TeAR improves success under heating by 10-15% without on-robot fine-tuning.
