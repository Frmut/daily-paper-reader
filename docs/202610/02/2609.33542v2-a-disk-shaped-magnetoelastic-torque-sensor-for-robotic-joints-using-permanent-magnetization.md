## 摘要
在机器人领域，直接扭矩传感的需求日益增长，以实现仅靠电机电流估算扭矩无法满足的精确控制与交互。本文提出了一种新型盘状磁弹性扭矩传感器，其轴向尺寸紧凑，约为1厘米，适合集成于机器人关节中。该传感器采用四磁力计架构，通过测量受应力影响的狭窄磁化区域所调制的磁场，同时抑制寄生交叉力的影响。定制设计的磁屏蔽装置在提高扭矩灵敏度的同时，将外部杂散磁场降低了7倍。该设备在50 Nm满量程（FS）下，扭矩测量精度达到1.34% FS。本文详细介绍了该传感器的开发过程，包括机械设计、磁化工艺及实验验证。结果表明，该传感器在机器人应用中具有广阔的应用前景。

## Abstract
Direct torque sensing is a growing need in the robotics community to enable precise control and interactions where torque estimation from motor current is not sufficient. This paper presents a novel disk-shaped magnetoelastic torque sensor with a compact axial envelope of about 1 cm, suitable for integration in robotic joints. A four-magnetometer architecture is used to measure the field modulated by the stress affecting a narrow magnetized region while rejecting the effects of parasitic cross forces. A custom-designed magnetic shield enhances the torque sensitivity while reducing the external stray fields by a factor 7x. The device measures the torque with an accuracy of 1.34 %FS relative to the 50 Nm full scale (FS). The paper details the development of the sensor through the mechanical design, the magnetization procedure, and the experimental validation. The results demonstrate the potential of the proposed sensor for robotic applications.
