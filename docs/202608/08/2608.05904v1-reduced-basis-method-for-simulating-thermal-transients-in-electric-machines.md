## 摘要
约化基 (RB) 方法为具有可控精度的参数化偏微分方程提供低维近似。我们讨论了用于感应电机瞬态热模拟的 RB 近似构建，并在经过测量校准后，将其性能与有限元 (FE) 模型和集总参数热网络 (LPTN) 进行了比较。数值结果表明，RB 模型能够达到 FE 的精度，支持全自动构建，并提供与传统 LPTN 相当的计算效率。

## Abstract
Reduced Basis (RB) methods provide low-dimensional approximations of parametrized partial differential equations with controllable accuracy. We discuss the construction of RB approximations for transient thermal simulation of an induction motor and compare their performance to Finite Element (FE) models and Lumped Parameter Thermal Networks (LPTNs) after calibration to measurements. Numerical results demonstrate that RB models can achieve FE accuracy, allow for a fully automatic construction, and offer computational efficiency comparable to traditional LPTNs.
