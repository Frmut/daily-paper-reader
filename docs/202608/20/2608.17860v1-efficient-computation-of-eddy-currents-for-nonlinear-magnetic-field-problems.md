## 摘要
估算电气设备导电非叠片部件中的涡流损耗需要昂贵的三维仿真。因此，在实际应用中，为了降低早期设计阶段的计算成本，通常采用各种近似方法。我们回顾了一些方法，并讨论了它们的建模假设及由此产生的近似。特别地，我们确定涡流反应场是一项重要的贡献，应当在全局范围内予以考虑。这些反应场可以通过求解单个线性化时周性问题，从二维静磁仿真中近似重构。我们进一步讨论了解决该后处理问题的不同策略。数值结果表明，与标准后处理相比，该方法在增加适度额外成本的情况下，提高了损耗预测的准确性。

## Abstract
Estimation of eddy-current losses in conducting non-laminated components of electrical devices requires expensive three-dimensional simulations. Various approximations are therefore used in practice to reduce the computational cost in the early design phase. We review some approaches and discuss their modelling assumptions and resulting approximations. In particular, we identify eddy-current reaction fields as a significant contribution that should be accounted for globally. These reaction fields can be approximately reconstructed from two-dimensional magnetostatic simulations by solving a single linearized time-periodic problem. We further discuss different strategies for solving this post-processing problem. Numerical results demonstrate improved loss prediction compared to standard post-processing at moderate additional cost.
