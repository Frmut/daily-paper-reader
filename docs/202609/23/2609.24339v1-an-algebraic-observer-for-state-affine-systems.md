## 摘要
我们提出了一种设计非线性状态仿射系统代数交换引理观测器的新型系统化方法。该观测器具有四个主要特点：（i）相比标准的渐近收敛观测器，其瞬态性能显著提升；（ii）结构简单，其构建仅依赖于基础线性滤波和单一矩阵求逆；（iii）适用于经典方法无法处理的非一致完全可观测系统；（iv）可直接扩展至未知时变参数和测量延迟的情况。本文的核心思想是将基于广义参数估计的观测器构建与简单的滤波技术相结合，从而推导出该代数观测器。研究表明，该设计适用于满足弱通用条件的一类状态仿射系统。此外，对于时不变系统，该条件始终全局成立。

## Abstract
We present a new systematic method to design an algebraic swapping lemma observer for nonlinear systems which are affine in the state. Four are the main features of the new observer: (i) Far superior transient performance compared to standard asymptotically convergent observers; (ii) structural simplicity - its construction relies solely on basic linear filtering and a single matrix inversion; (iii) applicability to non-uniformly-completely-observable systems, for which classical methods are not applicable; (iv) straightforward extension to the cases of unknown time-varying parameters and measurement delay. The core idea exploited in the paper is to combine the construction of a generalized parameter estimation-based observer with a simple filtering technique to derive the algebraic observer. The resulting design is shown to apply to a class of state-affine systems satisfying a weak generic condition. Moreover, it is established that for time-invariant systems, this condition is always satisfied globally.
