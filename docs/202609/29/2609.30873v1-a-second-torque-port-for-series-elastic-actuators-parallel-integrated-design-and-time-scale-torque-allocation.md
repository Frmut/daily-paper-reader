## 摘要
串联弹性驱动器（SEA）仅有一个转矩端口，但为此付出了双重代价：齿轮电机必须通过弹簧带动其自身的反射惯量，导致其输出幅值在饱和后随指令频率 ω 以 ω⁻² 的速度衰减，且低于传动系统静摩擦力的指令完全无法传递。本文在负载侧开辟了第二个转矩端口，将一个无框直驱微型电机与固定刚度弹簧并联，构成了一种并联集成式串联弹性驱动器（Pi-SEA）。该驱动器无需传感器，即可通过弹簧形变和微型电机电流读取输出转矩，并按时间尺度而非滤波器设计将指令转矩分配至两个通道。微型电机转矩环作为快速子系统，使闭环系统呈现奇异摄动特性，从而将通道分离需求转化为可校验的边界条件，而非需要调节的交叉频率；通过一个带泄漏的中程积分器将稳态负载回馈至弹簧；此外，将幅值上限反向推导，得出了一种闭式选型规则，可根据应用所需的幅值和频率对弹簧、齿轮电机及微型电机进行匹配。与传统 SEA 相比，Pi-SEA 在小幅值下的跟踪带宽更宽，且关节对环境产生的残余力矩降低了一个数量级。

## Abstract
A series elastic actuator has a single torque port and pays for it twice: the geared motor must swing its own reflected inertia through the spring, so the amplitude it delivers collapses as $ω^{-2}$ in the command frequency $ω$ once it saturates, while commands below the transmission's breakaway friction never arrive at all. This letter opens a second torque port on the load side, placing a frameless direct-drive micro motor in parallel with a fixed-stiffness spring -- a parallel-integrated SEA, or Pi-SEA, whose delivered torque is read from spring deflection and micro current without a sensor -- and dividing the commanded torque between the two channels by time scale rather than by filter design. The micro torque loop is the fast subsystem, which makes the closed loop singularly perturbed and turns the separation the channels need into a bound to check rather than a crossover to tune; a leaky mid-ranging integrator returns the steady load to the spring; and the amplitude ceiling, read backwards, becomes a closed-form sizing rule that matches spring, geared motor and micro motor to the amplitudes and frequencies an application asks for. Against SEAs, the Pi-SEA widens the tracked band at small amplitudes and lowers the residual the joint imposes on its environment, each by an order of magnitude.
